Stream-Flaflix

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Stream-Flaflix is self-hosted software. The operator of this instance controls the data stored in it and decides whether to connect Google Drive.

Google Drive access

When the operator connects Google Drive, Stream-Flaflix uses the authorized Google account only to create, list, download and delete its own encrypted .sqlite.zip backup files in the folder the operator selected. It does not read, modify or share other Google Drive files.

Credentials and backups

The OAuth client secret and refresh token are stored encrypted at rest with the server's SECRET_KEY. Short-lived access tokens are held in memory for uploads. Backup archives are encrypted with BACKUP_ENCRYPTION_KEY before they leave the server; that key remains on the server and with the operator.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties. It is not used to train AI models. Stream-Flaflix's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Drive copies follow the retention setting in the dashboard. The operator can delete them from the backup screen or revoke the app's access from the Google Account third-party connections page.
